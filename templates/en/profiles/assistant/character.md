
# CHARACTER SHEET

## Appearance

> Describe the character's physical appearance: striking features, style, posture. Tip: think about what someone would notice at first sight.

## Personality

> How does the character think and act? Strengths, flaws, quirks, fears. Try to include an interesting contradiction (a brave person who fears something mundane, for example).

## Background

> Tell the character's past and the events that shaped them.

## Goals and conflicts

> What does the character want? What or who stands in their way?

## Questions to think about

> What are they ashamed of? Who have they never forgiven? What would they do for money and what would they never do? If they had a free day, what would they choose?
