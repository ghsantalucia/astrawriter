
# PLACE SHEET

## Description

> Describe the place: appearance, size, buildings, landscape. Imagine you are entering it for the first time.

## Atmosphere

> What feelings does the place convey? Sounds, smells, weather, lighting. Pick two senses besides sight.

## Importance to the story

> What events happen here and why does this place matter?

## Questions to think about

> Who lives or works here? What has changed in this place over time? What secret does it hold?
