
# SYSTEM SHEET

## How it works

> Explain how the system works within the story's universe (magic, technology, religion, politics...). Try to explain it as if to a friend who has never read the story.

## Rules and limits

> What is possible and what is impossible? What are the restrictions? A good limit makes for good stories.

## Costs and consequences

> What is paid to use it? What effects does it have on the world and on people?

## Questions to think about

> Who benefits and who loses out? How could someone bend the rules? How does an ordinary character see it in daily life?
