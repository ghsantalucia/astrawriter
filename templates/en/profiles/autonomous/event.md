
# EVENT SHEET

## One-line summary

> What happened, when, and with whom, in one sentence.

## What happened

> Objective description of the facts, in the order they occurred.

## Consequences

> Direct and indirect effects on the story and the characters.

## Who knows what

> Who witnessed it, who knows only the official version, and who knows nothing. The AI must not let a character know what they could not.

## Instructions for the AI

> How this event may be mentioned (by whom, in what tone) and what must not yet be revealed to the reader.
