
# CHARACTER SHEET

## One-line summary

> Who they are, in one objective sentence: role in the story and dominant trait.

## Appearance

> Objective, permanent physical traits (hair, height, scars, clothing). The AI must keep these facts throughout the book.

## Personality

> Short list of dominant traits, flaws, and predictable behaviors under pressure.

## Voice and dialogue samples

> 3 to 5 sample lines and the speech rules: vocabulary, sentence length, what they would never say.

## Goals and conflicts

> Main goal, secret goal, and what stands in the way of each.

## Immutable facts

> Facts the AI can never contradict: age, family ties, origin, abilities, and limits.

## Instructions for the AI

> How to use this character: when they appear, what to reveal and what to hide, how to run their scenes.
