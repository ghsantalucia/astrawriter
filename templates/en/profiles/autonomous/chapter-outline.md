
# OUTLINE - CHAPTER

## Briefing for the AI

> **Chapter goal:** what needs to change between the beginning and the end.
> **Tone and point of view:** narrative person, tense, and mood.
> **Desired length:** approximate word count.

## Scenes (in order)

> For each scene: place, characters present, what happens, and how it ends.

## Clues to plant or pay off

> Ideas from the plan that must appear (or be resolved) in this chapter.

## Constraints

> What the AI must NOT do in this chapter (forbidden reveals, personality changes, deaths, premature endings).

## Done criteria

> Conditions the chapter must meet to be accepted.
