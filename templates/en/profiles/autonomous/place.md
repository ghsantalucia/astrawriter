
# PLACE SHEET

## One-line summary

> What the place is and what it is for in the story.

## Fixed description

> Physical facts that never change (location, size, buildings, landscape). The AI must keep them in all scenes.

## Mandatory sensory details

> Three or four concrete details (sounds, smells, light) the AI must include when describing the place.

## Importance to the story

> Events that happen here and the place's role in the plot.

## Instructions for the AI

> What tone to use in scenes here, who may be present, and what must never happen in this place.
