
# EVENT SHEET

## What happened

> The event in a few lines: who, where, and what changed in the story.

## Consequences

> What this event caused or made possible.

## Who knows what

> Who witnessed it, who only heard about it, and which version each group knows. The AI uses this so a character does not know what they could not.

## How it is remembered

> How the characters talk about this event day to day (nicknames, legends, taboos).
