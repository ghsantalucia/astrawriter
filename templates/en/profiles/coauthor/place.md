
# PLACE SHEET

## Description

> Appearance, size, buildings, landscape.

## Atmosphere

> Sounds, smells, weather, lighting, and the feeling the place conveys.

## Importance to the story

> What events happen here and why this place matters.

## Details the AI should repeat

> Three or four concrete sensory details (the creaking door, the smell of sea air...) that give the place its identity whenever it appears.

## Who frequents it

> Characters and groups who live, work, or pass through here, and how they are treated.
