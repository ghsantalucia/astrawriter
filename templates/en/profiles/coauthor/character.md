
# CHARACTER SHEET

## Appearance

> Striking physical traits, style, posture.

## Personality

> Strengths, flaws, quirks, fears, and how they react under pressure.

## Background

> The character's past and the events that shaped them.

## Goals and conflicts

> What they want, what stands in their way, and what they hide.

## Voice and dialogue samples

> Write 3 to 5 typical lines for them (cheerful, angry, lying, doubtful...). The AI uses these samples to write the character with the same voice every time.

## Way of speaking

> Vocabulary, slang, accent, sentence length, verbal tics. What they would never say.

## Relationships

> How they treat each important character: allies, rivals, family.
