# Welcome to Astra Writer

Here you write books with an AI assistant by your side, in a place that keeps your universe **organized and consistent**: characters, places, world rules, events and chapters, all linked together.

This book, the **User Manual**, is your guided tour: each file in it explains what that kind of file is. The **blue** links open other files in this manual; the ones that lead to an **app window** (settings, centers) open the window right away, just click.

## Your first steps

1. **Connect an AI.** Until you connect one, the chat answers only with the *built-in assistant*, which is not a real AI. Open the [AI Center, Accounts and model tab](app:central/ai/model), choose a service (Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, Groq or a model on your computer, such as Ollama) and paste your key. Want to spend less? See the [AI usage profile](app:central/ai/analysis).
2. **Create your book.** At the top of the sidebar, click the book name and choose **New book**. You can also **import** a book you exported earlier.
3. **Write.** In **Writing**, create the first chapter. What you write stays as a draft (an asterisk next to the name) until you press **Sync**.
4. **Build your world.** In **World**, create sheets for characters, places, concepts and events. The more the AI knows, the more it helps.

## Quick tips

- **Ctrl+Shift+Y** opens the System Center; the **Esc** key closes windows and drawers, one at a time.
- Everything the AI changes becomes a **draft**: you compare and decide what stays. Nothing is overwritten without your approval (in autonomous mode, everything is recorded in the [changelog](app:central/book/changelog) and can be undone).
- Your books stay on your computer, in **Documents > Astra Writer**. Nothing goes to the cloud, except what you send to the AI you choose.
- When you are done exploring, create your book and delete this one if you like: it is only a guide.

Happy working, and happy storytelling!
